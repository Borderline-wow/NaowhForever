# PvP

What your target is doing in a fight. **PvP Auras** shows your target's short buffs (Divine
Shield, Evasion, Ice Block, Blessing of Protection, Sprint), the crowd control on them and the
debuffs you pick, as rows of large icons; a second panel does the same for your focus, with
their name and class above it, so a Sap or Polymorph on one enemy keeps counting down while you
fight another. **Battleground Displays** puts the game's battleground scores and start
countdown in the HUD Editor. Off by default (`enabled` in the `pvp` store, from
`ns.FEATURES.pvp`).

## Layout

```
NaowhForever_PvP/
  NaowhForever_PvP.toc       the addon; lists only PvP.xml
  PvP.xml                    every file, in load order
  Data/
    Spells.lua               crowd control by ability, other crowd control, PvP debuffs, by
                             spell ID (ns.PvPSpells), generated by Tools/build/pvp_auras.py
  PvP.lua                    the settings (ns.PvPSettings) and the module table (ns.PvP, P)
  AuraRows.lua               PvP Auras' panels and rows, and the buff, crowd control and debuff
                             filters they pick by (P.Displays, P.RowOn, P.RefreshLists)
  View/
    Style.lua                its look (P.Style), on top of Shared/Style.lua
    Icon.lua                 an aura icon and its time left, on an aura button or the preview (P.Icon)
    AuraPanel.lua            a PvP Auras panel on Blizzard's AuraContainer: its rows, the absorb and
                             the focus's name (P.Panel)
  Auras.lua                  PvP Auras shown, hidden and kept up to date: events, combat, Unlock Mode
  Displays.lua               the battleground scores and countdown, movable in the HUD Editor
  UI/
    AurasPreview.lua         the PvP Auras card's preview with sample auras (P.AurasPreview)
    SpellGrid.lua            the crowd control and debuff grids, one icon per spell (P.SpellGrid)
    AurasPage.lua            the PvP/Auras settings page, declared as cards
    BattlegroundsPage.lua    the PvP/Battlegrounds settings page
  README.md                  this file
```

Each crowd control ability and each debuff in the spell data has its own switch:
`cc_<key>`, on by default, and `debuff_<key>`, off.

## Why

- The spell data loads before `PvP.lua`: the per-spell switches' defaults are built from it.
- Forever closes auras to addons in combat, so Blizzard's AuraContainer picks and draws them;
  the addon only hands it filters.
- Forever's classic spells carry none of the game's PvP aura flags, and the AuraContainer lets
  an addon pick an enemy's debuffs by spell ID but not their buffs: crowd control and debuffs
  come from spell IDs, buffs by their length (`buffLength`) and dispel type (Magic only).
- A container does not follow a new target or focus by itself, so a change rebuilds it
  (`UpdateAllAuras`).
- Each list's signature says which switches were on, so an unchanged choice is not handed to
  the container again; its version tells each panel it has a new one.
- Nothing is shown, hidden or resized in combat: settings changed then wait for
  `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED`.
- While your focus is also your target the focus panel fades out by alpha (safe in combat), the
  target panel already showing it all. Where the game will not say whether the two are one (a
  secret value), it stays.
- The time left is the game's own countdown, coloured by a curve the game evaluates: white,
  yellow under twice `warnAt`, red under `warnAt`, and blinking every `BLINK` (half a second)
  while red. The preview paints the curve's bright half itself.
- The absorb is the shields on the unit added up. The game hands the amount over secret, so it
  is shown as given, never compared: the text sits in a frame clipped to a bar that is full
  while any shield is up and empty without one, so the number shows only while there is a
  shield. A small shield icon before it, the same on every panel, says what it is.
- NF Focus, the Macros module's focus macro, is how a player sets their focus: Open Forge opens
  Naowh's Forge on its Smart Macros, where it can be dragged to a bar.
- Battleground Displays: until one is dragged its holder follows the game's frame, which stays
  where the game (or the Top Bar) put it; once dragged, the game's frame hangs from the holder.
  `SCORES_Y` (-15) is the game's own spot for the scores.
- The countdown's bars hang from the top of a screen-sized frame and its last seconds sit at
  its centre, so the whole frame moves with the first bar (`TIMER_TOP`, one bar under the top
  of the screen), and is sized again when the UI scale or the screen changes.

## Checking

- `lua Tools/regression/test-pvp-auras.lua`: the spell lists, nothing made or listened to while
  off, the rows and filters, the focus panel, the absorb, the preview and the spell grids.
- `lua Tools/regression/test-pvp-displays.lua`: the battleground displays, off and on, dragged
  and reset.
